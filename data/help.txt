[comb]
When two mics hear one speaker from different distances, their signals arrive a fraction of a millisecond apart.
Where they meet out of phase they cancel, leaving a row of notches: a comb.

On a bass cab the notches land in the low mids and mids, from a few hundred Hz to a couple of kHz. That is where a
bass is actually heard in a mix: its growl and the definition of each note. So the tone turns hollow, and some notes
speak less than others.

That is why Orbit Bass Cab Builder treats a comb as a fault. The response line turns red wherever the two mics cancel
by 3 dB or more. FIND BEST POSITION moves mic B to where the pair cancels least. Every factory preset keeps its pair
within about 3 dB across the whole range. ALIGN, on by default, lines the two mics up in time, which moves the first
notches up above about 3 kHz.
