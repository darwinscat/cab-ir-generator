# THE GUITAR CABINETS: [<cab> <position>] sections, `key = value`, `inherit` laid under left to right — the same keys as
# the bass's (data/cabinets.txt, src/synth/CabSynth.h). Our own boxes and speakers, not measurements of any one: tuned
# against the shape measured guitar cabinets share, close-miked at the cap (27 commercial packs, each IR levelled by its
# 400 Hz-2.5 kHz, 08.11) — a steep foot under 80 Hz (63 Hz some 7 dB under 1 kHz); a closed box's hump at 125-200 Hz
# some 5 dB over it, an open one's hardly any there and 2-3 dB at 250-400; flat to 5 kHz with a few deep dips in the
# cone's breakup (1.5-6 kHz: 3 to 7 dips of 5 to 10 dB, a dip near 1.1 kHz); a cliff from 5.5 kHz to a shelf some 25 dB
# under 1 kHz at 7.5-10 kHz, then falling 17 dB an octave (16 kHz some 42 dB under).

[defaults]

# A closed box's echo, out through the cone again (CabSynth.h, Cab::echoGain): 0.65 of it each way round, 400 Hz to
