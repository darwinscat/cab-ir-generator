[comb]
When two mics hear one speaker from different distances, their signals arrive a fraction of a millisecond apart.
Where they meet out of phase they cancel, leaving a row of notches: a comb.

On a guitar cab a comb does two different things. Below about 2 kHz it hurts: palm mutes and chugs go thin and loose.
Higher up it is a classic tone tool. Engineers nudge the second mic so the pair notches out fizz or adds a phase colour
that EQ can't quite copy.

Orbit Guitar Cab Builder follows that split. Below 2 kHz the pair may cancel by at most 3 dB. From 2 to 12 kHz it may
cancel by up to 6 dB before the response line turns red. FIND BEST POSITION judges by the same rule, and ALIGN, on by
default, lines the two mics up in time. Some metal, modern metal and hard rock presets keep a light comb in the top on
purpose.
